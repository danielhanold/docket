## The deliberate brevity-bound raise, 25 → 45

The brevity assert exists because this block rides always-loaded context in every harness. The build
raised its bound from 25 to 43 lines; review raised it again to **45** (commit `97e949a4`, which
retitled the two detached branches by the state that discriminates them rather than by launch
shape). **45 is the bound of record.** Against the original 25, that grows the block by 80%, and
that is a cost a future reader is entitled to see **priced here** rather than discover by
measuring. The raise is recorded in the
test's own history comment as well as here; the assert was not deleted, only re-bounded, so the
next author still has to justify the next line.

The bound raise is why the pre-implementation RED reading matters more than usual: with the guards
written and the template untouched, exactly **13** asserts went `NOT OK` — 2 header-window, 2
step-2, and all 9 detached. The brevity assert stayed **green**, because the raised bound (43 then,
45 now) is *weaker* than 25 and a weaker bound cannot fail on the old file. Both verbatim-render
asserts and the currency assert also stayed green for the same structural reason. That green trio is the reason the
bound got its own dedicated mutation probe below.

**Added by review (commit `1590cc25`, asserts 53 → 56).** Review found the two detached branches
were not actually pinned apart, and added three further branch-separation probes: **M5a** a bullet
merge (the two branch bullets collapsed into one), **M5b** migration of the outcome clause from one
branch to the other, and **M5c** hoisting `**Never re-dispatch**` out of the unattributed branch.

The reviewer's suggested per-branch window slicers were **insufficient on their own**: `flat()`
collapses the newline and the `- ` bullet marker into a single space, so a pure bullet merge (M5a)
left both per-branch windows byte-identical to their pre-mutation text and every assert stayed
green. What closes that leg is a **bullet-shape count taken on the raw template**, keyed on
`^- [*][*]` — it sees the marker that `flat()` erases. M5b and M5c are what the per-branch slicers
do catch, and they redden only with the slicers in place.

**Known coupling, not a finding:** `this repo's committed AGENTS.md block is current` reddens during
every one of these probes, because the assembler splices the *mutated* template while the committed
block holds the real one. It is green in the committed state.

## Deliberate non-goals

- **`cursor-rules/dispatch.head.md` was left alone on purpose.** Its item 2 carries the same "never
  background it and never poll" sentence, and on the Cursor surface it is spliced above the gate —
  but its directive governs **every** docket agent dispatch, not just implement-next runs. Narrowing
  it would have loosened an unrelated rule. The Detached section instead self-scopes in its own
  heading, so the gate is self-consistent on both surfaces from its own text.
- **No polling loop.** The detached path regains control exactly once, at the notification, and it
  deliberately grows no observation loop: change 0286's `gate-run --observe` loop shape is not
  imported here.
- **Native-dispatch-only.** Change 0277's `--brief-file` channel does not reach this gate, and no
  prose added here is written as a facade invocation with trailing argv.
