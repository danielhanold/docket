## What was built

run.

## The deliberate brevity-bound raise, 25 → 43

The brevity assert exists because this block rides always-loaded context in every harness. Raising
its bound from 25 to 43 lines grows that block by 72%, and that is a cost a future reader is
entitled to see **priced here** rather than discover by measuring. The raise is recorded in the
test's own history comment as well as here; the assert was not deleted, only re-bounded, so the
next author still has to justify the next line.

The bound raise is why the pre-implementation RED reading matters more than usual: with the guards
written and the template untouched, exactly **13** asserts went `NOT OK` — 2 header-window, 2
step-2, and all 9 detached. The brevity assert stayed **green**, because 43 is a *weaker* bound
than 25 and a weaker bound cannot fail on the old file. Both verbatim-render asserts and the
currency assert also stayed green for the same structural reason. That green trio is the reason the
bound got its own dedicated mutation probe below.

## Mutation evidence

Every probe used a `cp` backup and an `mv -f` restore — never `git checkout --`, which would have
destroyed the uncommitted edit under test — and every mutation was proven landed with `grep -c` or
a non-empty `diff` before its result was interpreted.

- **Bound probe.** Appended one line; 43 → 44 confirmed. Red: `gate text is at most 43 lines`.
  The verbatim-render asserts did **not** redden, because `slice_gate` reads `GATE_LINES` and both
  sides move together. Worth recording explicitly: the plan predicted they might, and they do not.
- **M1 — whole section removed.** `Detached dispatch` occurrences 2 → 1, file 43 → 29 lines. Red:
  all nine detached asserts. This confirms the `DETACHED` window is non-vacuous — with the heading
  gone the window collapses to the whole file, and the phrase asserts fail rather than quietly
  matching the same words somewhere else.
- **M2 — epoch conjunct only.** Deleting just the conjunct added at the design gate reddened
  `detached branch A requires all three filters, named` and **only** that assert from the branch-A
  group. The widened clause is guarded per-conjunct, not riding the pair.
- **M3 — permissive twin.** `**Never re-dispatch** here: that needs all` → `You may re-dispatch
  here once, using all`. Red: `unattributed mode never re-dispatches, and says why a timestamp
  cannot attribute`.
- **M4 — blanket step 2 restored.** Red on both new step-2 asserts, while the pre-existing
  `step 2 forbids backgrounding and polling on that path` stayed **green**. That is precisely why
  the two new asserts had to be written: the old assert cannot tell a blanket prohibition from a
  scoped one.

**Known coupling, not a finding:** `this repo's committed AGENTS.md block is current` reddens during
every one of these probes, because the assembler splices the *mutated* template while the committed
block holds the real one. It is green in the committed state.

## Deliberate non-goals

- **`cursor-rules/dispatch.head.md` was left alone on purpose.** Its item 2 carries the same "never
  background it and never poll" sentence, and on the Cursor surface it is spliced above the gate —
  but its directive governs **every** docket agent dispatch, not just implement-next runs. Narrowing
  it would have loosened an unrelated rule. The Detached section instead self-scopes in its own
  heading, so the gate is self-consistent on both surfaces from its own text.
- **No polling loop.** The detached path regains control exactly once, at the notification, and it
  deliberately grows no observation loop: change 0286's `gate-run --observe` loop shape is not
  imported here.
- **Native-dispatch-only.** Change 0277's `--brief-file` channel does not reach this gate, and no
  prose added here is written as a facade invocation with trailing argv.
