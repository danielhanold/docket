Both exemption mechanisms the design relies on are live — `tests/test_docket_build.sh`'s `:!docs/results` pathspec exclusion and `tests/test_readme_finalize_docs.sh`'s `--glob "!docs/results/**"` escape — and the shipped guard now classifies a **57-occurrence** corpus (design time estimated ~38) at **hazard 0**, plus **83 whole-tree walk sites** at **hazard 0**. The degrade-off rule never fired: the property holds, so the limb ships armed in this repo and default-off everywhere else.

**Whole-branch review returned 12 findings (1 blocker, 4 important, 7 minor); all 12 are dispositioned in the PR body.** Three are worth recording beyond that table:

- **The blocker was a real smuggling vector, not a prose nit.** The original derivation `git diff --name-only -z <head_sha>..HEAD` relies on git's default rename detection, which emits only the *destination* path of a rename pair. A post-gate `git mv scripts/foo.sh <results_dir>/foo.sh` would therefore read as a 100% docs-only delta, fire the skip, and merge a branch whose suite composition changed after the gate. Fixed by `--no-renames` and pinned by a co-presence assert. Recorded in ADR-0066's update note because it is the kind of default that will look incidental to the next reader.
- **The degrade-off rule was unreachable prose as originally written.** Nothing distributes the guard test to consumer repos and nothing gated on it, so a merge-gating skip would have shipped on-by-default to every downstream repo on a property verified for exactly one. This became the `finalize.skip_results_only_delta` config key — the single largest deviation from the plan (below).
- **The guard's hazard predicate was a hand-written verb list**, the exact shape the repo's own `backstop-must-compute-not-reenumerate` learning warns against. It was **inverted** so benignity must be positively established: every executable, un-negated, non-assignment occurrence is HAZARD unless it matches a budgeted benign shape or a curated exemption. Measured false-positive rate on the live corpus was 3 of 57, all three declared in a separately-budgeted table.

**ADR:** ADR-0066 gained a dated `## Update` (2026-08-07, change 0190) recording the trust boundary moving from "state provably identical" to "state provably identical **or** delta provably suite-invisible", the consumer-side-over-producer-re-mint reasoning, the rejection of an attestation field, and the arming key.
